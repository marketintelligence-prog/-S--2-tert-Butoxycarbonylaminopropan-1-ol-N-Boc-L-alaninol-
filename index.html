Synonyms: N-Boc-L-alaninol; Boc-L-alaninol; Boc-Ala-ol; (S)-2-(Boc-amino)-1-propanol; (S)-(−)-2-(tert-butoxycarbonylamino)-1-propanol; N-(tert-butoxycarbonyl)-L-alaninol; tert-butyl [(1S)-2-hydroxy-1-methylethyl]carbamate [9].

Boc-L-alaninol is L-alaninol ((S)-2-amino-1-propanol) with its amine masked as a tert-butyl carbamate. That leaves a free primary alcohol that can be activated (mesylate, Mitsunobu, cyclic sulfamidate) while the stereocentre and nitrogen stay protected. Its commercial value comes from installing a chiral (S)-1-methyl-2-aminoethyl fragment. In darolutamide, a Mitsunobu reaction couples it with the aryl-pyrazole and Boc removal gives the amine (82%) [18]. Cyclising it with SOCl2 then RuCl3/NaIO4 gives the (S)-4-methyl cyclic sulfamidate, run at ~87 g scale in 90% yield in US 9,902,731 [16].

Industry Applications, End-Products & Application Market Sizes

A. Pharmaceutical intermediates — darolutamide (oncology)

Darolutamide (Nubeqa; Bayer/Orion) is N-((S)-1-(3-(3-chloro-4-cyanophenyl)-1H-pyrazol-1-yl)propan-2-yl)-5-(1-hydroxyethyl)-1H-pyrazole-3-carboxamide [19]. The (S)-propan-2-yl amine arm comes from Boc-L-alaninol. The pyrazole is joined by Mitsunobu, and the protonated amine is pulled into the aqueous phase to strip Mitsunobu by-products (82%) [18].

B. Pharmaceutical intermediates — orforglipron (obesity / T2D)

Orforglipron (Foundayo; Lilly, discovered by Chugai) is a non-peptide oral GLP-1 agonist, C48H48F2N10O5 [52]. It was approved for chronic weight management on 1-Apr-2026, the first new molecular entity cleared under the CNPV voucher pilot [20]. Pharmacompass and Blue Jet list Boc-L-alaninol as a KSM for orforglipron calcium hydrate [10][11].

C. Discovery chemistry, peptidomimetics & research standards

Catalogue suppliers position it as a chiral building block for peptide and peptidomimetic synthesis and inhibitor design [45][47]. Typical medicinal-chemistry uses:

  • Swern oxidation to Boc-L-alaninal.
  • Mitsunobu N-alkylation of heterocycles.
  • Cyclic sulfamidates as chiral 2-aminopropyl electrophiles that ring-open with inversion [49].

It is also sold as a characterised reference standard [46].

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL
The product links to two growing, high-value drugs: darolutamide (≈US$3.2 bn US alone) and orforglipron (approved Apr-2026). But the merchant KSM market is small (~12–25 MT/yr) and the chemistry is easy to copy. Enter now with low-capital modes (trading, sourcing, CRO derivatives) aimed at Indian darolutamide generic makers. Do not invest in dedicated manufacturing until (a) an orforglipron or darolutamide customer confirms ≥10 MT/yr offtake and (b) the orforglipron route is verified.
